Request account deletion

Use the email address and password for your Omalis account to verify your identity and submit a deletion request.

After you submit the request, there is a 7-day waiting period. You can cancel it from the Omalis app before deletion begins. When processing completes, your account and associated data will be deleted. Messages you sent to other people may be retained with your sender identity shown as “Deleted account.”
