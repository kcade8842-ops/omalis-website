Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Omalis is a Somali-first social video-sharing platform designed to help people share videos, discover creators, and connect with others. This policy explains how Omalis handles personal information when you use our application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Omalis, we may process the following information:

  • Account information: Email address, account identifier, username, display name, and profile details.
  • Google sign-in information: Information Google provides during authentication, such as your email address, name, and profile image, subject to your permissions and configuration.
  • User content: Videos, captions, comments, profile images, and other content you choose to upload or share.
  • Interactions: Likes, follows, messages, shared videos, and related activity needed to provide the application's features.
  • Technical information: Information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service, which may include device information, diagnostic records, and notification tokens.

The exact information collected depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate and protect Omalis.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain accounts and authenticate users.
  • Display profiles, videos, comments, likes, and follows.
  • Deliver messages and relevant notifications.
  • Respond to reports, investigate abuse, and protect users.
  • Maintain service reliability, prevent fraud, and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Videos and Public Information

Information you publish, such as videos, captions, comments, and profile details, may be visible to other users according to the application's features and settings. Other users may copy or share content you make available to them.

Do not publish private information about yourself or another person without an appropriate reason and permission.

4. Messages

Omalis may process messages and shared-video information to provide messaging, deliver content, and maintain service security. Messages should not be assumed to have end-to-end encryption. Do not send sensitive information through the application unless you understand the risks.

5. Service Providers and Sharing

Omalis uses third-party services to operate parts of the application. These may include Supabase for authentication, database and file-storage services, and Google Firebase services for push notifications. Google may also process information when you choose Google sign-in.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply. We do not sell personal information as a business model.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to address security risks, comply with applicable law, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

6. Storage and Security

Omalis uses third-party infrastructure to store and process account information and content. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Access to information should be limited to authorized purposes.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and its purpose.
